About Me

I'm a Propulsion Design Engineer and Computational Engineering Lead with a passion for solving complex engineering challenges. I specialize in propulsion system design, computational methods, and building simulation tools that accelerate the engineering workflow.

My work sits at the intersection of aerospace engineering and software development. Across my repositories I build custom solvers and SPH/CFD simulations, voxel-based 3D-printable geometry in C#, interactive Three.js and Streamlit interfaces, and automation ranging from Playwright browsers to Raspberry Pi Pico HID provisioning — turning raw physics into tools and actionable design insight.
